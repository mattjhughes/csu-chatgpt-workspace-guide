# AI Chat Tool Use Notice

AI Chat Tools may generate inaccurate, outdated, incomplete, or misleading information. Do not rely on them as a substitute for your own judgement, professional advice, medical advice, clinical care, crisis support, academic judgment, or official university guidance. Verify important information through appropriate and authoritative sources.

Your use of this tool must comply with the CSU Responsible Use Policy and any applicable campus policies. Review the policy here: [CSU Responsible Use Policy](https://calstate.policystat.com/policy/18976900/latest).

AI Chat Tools are not mental health professionals and should not be used to provide crisis counseling, diagnosis, or treatment. If you or someone else may be in immediate danger, call 911. If you are in emotional distress or need crisis support, call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
